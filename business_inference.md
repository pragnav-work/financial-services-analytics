# Business Inference – Financial Services Analytics

## 1. Project Overview

This document presents business insights derived from five years of synthetic financial services data covering January 2022 to December 2026.

The analysis focuses on six key performance indicators (KPIs):

- Assets Under Management (AUM)
- Investment Return (ROI)
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- Net Promoter Score (NPS)
- Loan Default Rate
- Customer Acquisition by Channel

The objective is to understand business performance, identify trends and seasonal variations, and highlight areas that may require management attention.

**Note:** The dataset is synthetically generated. The observations represent patterns within the simulated data and should not be interpreted as actual financial market or institutional performance.

---

## 2. Key Business Insights

### 2.1 Assets Under Management (AUM)

**Observation:**

- AUM increases consistently throughout the five-year period.
- It grows from approximately ₹1.96 lakh in Q1 2022 to ₹17.43 crore in Q4 2026.
- The growth becomes particularly noticeable from 2024 onward.

**Business Interpretation:**

The increasing AUM indicates expansion in the overall investment portfolio.

This could be associated with customer acquisition, additional investments, recurring contributions, and portfolio value changes.

**Business Recommendation:**

- Continue monitoring portfolio growth and customer investment contributions.
- Identify institutions and products contributing most to AUM growth.
- Monitor whether portfolio growth is accompanied by healthy investment returns.

### 2.2 Investment Return (ROI)

**Observation:**

- ROI declines from -3.47% in Q1 2022 to -37.32% in Q3 2026.
- It improves slightly to -36.10% in Q4 2026.
- Despite substantial AUM growth, the portfolio consistently remains below its invested amount.

**Business Interpretation:**

The declining ROI indicates that the combined market value of investments is increasingly below the total invested amount.

This creates an important contrast: the investment portfolio is growing in size, but its relative valuation performance is deteriorating.

**Business Recommendation:**

- Investigate investment performance across individual products and institutions.
- Identify products contributing most to negative returns.
- Review portfolio allocation and risk exposure.
- Monitor market value against invested capital over time.

### 2.3 Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

**Observation:**

- CAC fluctuates throughout the five-year period.
- Q1 generally records higher acquisition costs than other quarters.
- CAC reaches approximately ₹35,064 in Q1 2022.
- It falls to approximately ₹21,718 in Q2 2026.

**Business Interpretation:**

The recurring increase in Q1 acquisition costs is consistent with the seasonal variation introduced into the synthetic marketing and sales expense data.

Higher acquisition expenses do not always result in proportionately higher customer acquisition.

**Business Recommendation:**

- Evaluate the effectiveness of marketing and sales expenditure.
- Compare acquisition costs across channels and institutions.
- Investigate whether higher spending during Q1 generates sufficient new customers.
- Optimize spending towards channels with lower acquisition costs.

### 2.4 Net Promoter Score (NPS)

**Observation:**

- NPS fluctuates significantly throughout the analysis period.
- The highest quarterly score is +8.70 in Q2 2022.
- The lowest score is -36.36 in Q1 2022.
- NPS remains negative throughout most of the later observation period.

**Business Interpretation:**

Negative NPS indicates that detractors outnumber promoters.

Although customer acquisition remains relatively stable, customer satisfaction does not show consistent improvement.

This suggests that acquiring new customers alone may not be sufficient to ensure long-term customer loyalty.

**Business Recommendation:**

- Investigate recurring customer complaints and service issues.
- Improve customer support and issue resolution.
- Collect feedback across different products and institutions.
- Monitor NPS alongside customer retention and acquisition.

### 2.5 Loan Default Rate

**Observation:**

- Quarterly loan default rates fluctuate between approximately 7.46% and 15.94%.
- The highest observed rate is 15.94% in Q4 2024.
- The rate falls to 7.63% in Q3 2026 before increasing to 15.32% in Q4 2026.

**Business Interpretation:**

The fluctuations indicate inconsistent loan repayment outcomes across loan origination cohorts.

The increase in some quarters could indicate differences in borrower risk profiles or lending conditions.

However, the available data does not establish the actual cause of these variations.

**Business Recommendation:**

- Strengthen borrower eligibility and credit risk assessment.
- Monitor default rates across loan products and institutions.
- Identify customer segments associated with higher default rates.
- Track loan repayment performance over time.

### 2.6 Customer Acquisition by Channel

**Observation:**

- Quarterly customer acquisition generally remains between 234 and 281 customers.
- The highest acquisition is 281 customers in Q2 2026.
- Online acquisition is generally one of the largest contributors.
- Branch, referral, and social media channels also contribute consistently.

**Business Interpretation:**

The relatively stable acquisition numbers indicate consistent customer onboarding throughout the observation period.

Online acquisition plays an important role, but other channels continue to contribute meaningfully.

The channel mix changes across quarters, suggesting that customer acquisition sources are not equally consistent over time.

**Business Recommendation:**

- Continue evaluating online acquisition performance.
- Compare CAC across individual acquisition channels.
- Identify channels with the highest customer conversion rates.
- Invest in channels that provide sustainable customer growth.

## 3. Seasonal Trends

The generated dataset incorporates seasonal variations in certain business metrics.

The analysis highlights the following patterns:

| Metric | Observed Pattern |
|---|---|
| CAC | Generally higher during Q1 |
| NPS | Some fluctuations associated with simulated seasonal adjustments |
| AUM | Consistent long-term growth |
| ROI | Long-term declining trend |
| Loan Default | Fluctuates without a consistent seasonal pattern |
| Customer Acquisition | Relatively stable across quarters |

The higher Q1 CAC is consistent with the increased marketing and sales expenses introduced during January, February, and March in the data generation process.

These seasonal patterns are simulated assumptions rather than independently established real-world business trends.

---

## 4. Overall Business Assessment

The analysis reveals several important business observations:

1. **Portfolio Growth:** AUM demonstrates substantial growth over five years, indicating expansion in the overall investment portfolio.

2. **Investment Performance:** Despite AUM growth, ROI deteriorates significantly, highlighting a potential investment performance concern.

3. **Acquisition Efficiency:** CAC fluctuates and shows recurring Q1 increases, suggesting opportunities to optimize acquisition spending.

4. **Customer Satisfaction:** Predominantly negative NPS indicates that customer experience requires attention.

5. **Credit Risk:** Loan default rates fluctuate considerably, highlighting the importance of continuous credit risk monitoring.

6. **Customer Growth:** Customer acquisition remains relatively stable, with online channels making a significant contribution.

## 5. Conclusion

The financial services analytics project demonstrates how data analytics can help monitor institutional performance across investment management, customer acquisition, customer satisfaction, and lending operations.

The most significant finding is the contrast between increasing AUM and declining ROI. While the overall investment portfolio expands considerably, its relative performance deteriorates over the observation period.

Additionally, negative NPS and fluctuating loan default rates highlight areas requiring further investigation.

By monitoring these KPIs together, financial institutions can identify operational weaknesses, evaluate business performance, and make more informed strategic decisions.

---

**Data and Methodology Notes**

- All observations are based on synthetically generated data covering January 2022 to December 2026.
- Quarterly AUM and ROI represent the latest available monthly portfolio snapshots within each quarter.
- ROI compares market value against invested capital; it is not the individual quarter's investment return.
- CAC is calculated as total acquisition expenditure divided by the number of newly acquired customers.
- NPS is calculated using promoters (ratings 9–10), passives (7–8), and detractors (0–6).
- Loan default rate is calculated using the current loan status grouped by loan origination quarter. It does not represent the proportion of loans that defaulted during that particular quarter.
- Seasonal patterns are partly influenced by assumptions introduced during synthetic data generation.
